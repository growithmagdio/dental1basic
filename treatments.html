Clinical Disciplines

Treatments crafted with biological respect.

Every smile is an irreplaceable piece of human anatomy. We blend master ceramic craftsmanship, digital diagnostics, and gentle touch to deliver enduring health and natural beauty.

Aesthetic Dentistry

Hand-Layered Porcelain Veneers

Individually crafted by master dental ceramists using microscopic layering to match your natural facial symmetry and light transmission.

Unlike conventional veneers that look artificially opaque or chalky, our veneers are created with varying opacities and subtle incisal translucency. We practice conservative minimal-prep technique to preserve tooth vitality.

Ideal for: Chipped, worn, discolored, or uneven teeth
Treatment time: 2 to 3 appointments over 3 weeks
Investment guide: From £1,150 per tooth

Orthodontics

Bespoke Clear Aligners

Discreet orthodontic realignment planned tooth-by-tooth with 3D digital kinematics.

Clear aligners offer a comfortable, virtually invisible way to correct crowding, gaps, and bite misalignment. With digital remote monitoring, you enjoy fewer in-clinic appointments with optimal precision.

Ideal for: Crowding, spacing, relapse, and bite correction
Treatment time: Typically 4 to 9 months
Investment guide: From £2,800 (Full Course)

Enamel-Safe Whitening

Gentle, clinically supervised brightening using desensitising potassium nitrate and cool blue LED activation.

Eliminates coffee, tea, and natural age-related discoloration without stripping minerals or causing sharp sensitivity. Includes custom-sculpted overnight maintenance trays.

Duration: 90-minute in-studio session
Investment: £595 (Includes custom take-home kit)

Biomimetic Dental Implants

Ideal for: Single or multiple missing teeth, failing root canals
