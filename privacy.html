Privacy Policy

ℹ️ Demonstration Website Notice: Lumora Dental Studio is a fictional luxury dental practice prototype. No personal, financial, or medical data is transmitted, permanently stored, or shared with third parties.

1. Data Confidentiality & Medical Discretion

In a clinical private practice setting, patient privacy and diagnostic records are safeguarded under strict medical confidentiality guidelines (including UK GDPR and Data Protection Act standards). On this demonstration website, all interactions are executed locally within the client browser.

2. Information We Simulate

Information entered into the consultation request forms (such as names, mock contact numbers, and appointment notes) is parsed by client-side JavaScript solely to render the demonstration confirmation modal and does not leave your local device.

3. Clinical Photography & Radiographs

All photographic assets on this site are either synthetically generated or artistic concept demonstrations. Real patients’ photographic records would require formal written consent prior to any publication.
