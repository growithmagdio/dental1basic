Clarity & Confidence

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Lumora Dental Studio.

Your initial visit is an unhurried 60-minute appointment. We begin in our private consultation suite with a 3D digital scan, low-dose digital photography, and an in-depth conversation regarding your aesthetic goals and health history. You will leave with a written, fully transparent roadmap.

Our entire practice is designed as an architectural sensory retreat. Suites are soundproofed, illuminated with soft diffuse lighting, and free of visible medical clutter. We offer ceiling-mounted entertainment, custom organic aromatherapy, noise-cancelling headphones, and optional conscious sedation if requested.

We adhere strictly to biomimetic dentistry: preserving every fraction of healthy natural enamel possible. Whenever clinically feasible, we use ultra-conservative, micro-prep or non-prep techniques measuring only 0.2mm to 0.4mm, ensuring optimal adhesion and biological longevity.

Treatment durations vary based on individual biology and complexity, but most mild to moderate cosmetic alignment treatments complete within 4 to 9 months. We use digital monitoring apps allowing you to check in remotely between in-clinic visits.

Yes. For patients desiring complete privacy and convenience, our Mayfair location features private off-street courtyard access and complimentary valet parking upon prior arrangement with our concierge team.
